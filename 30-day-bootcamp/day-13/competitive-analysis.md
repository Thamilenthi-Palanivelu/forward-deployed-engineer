## How to Present This to the Client

- **Do** share this analysis openly. Transparency builds trust.
- **Do** acknowledge where competitors are strong. Clients know you are biased -- honesty stands out.
- **Do** focus on the client's specific context, not abstract comparisons.
- **Do NOT** trash competitors. It makes you look insecure.
- **Do NOT** pretend doing nothing has zero appeal. For some organizations, it is the rational choice (for now).
