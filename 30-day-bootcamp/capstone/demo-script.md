# Capstone Demo Script

**Date:** *YYYY-MM-DD*
**Audience:** *Describe your audience -- e.g., technical leads, executives, mixed*

*Follow this script for your capstone demo. Total time: 15-20 minutes.*

---

## Opening (90 seconds -- Business Context)

*Set the stage. Who is the customer? What problem are they facing? Why does it matter?*

> *"Thank you for joining today. I've been working with [Customer Name] in the [industry] space. They're facing [specific problem], which is costing them [quantified impact] per [time period]. Today I'll show you the solution we built to address this, and walk through the results we've achieved so far."*

*Transition: "Let me show you how it works."*

---

## Live Demo

1. **Step 1: *Show the starting point***
   - **What to Show:** *The initial screen, dashboard, or input interface*
   - **What to Say:** *"This is where a user begins. They see [describe the view] and their first action is to [describe action]."*
   - **Transition:** *"Now let's walk through the core workflow."*

2. **Step 2: *Demonstrate data input***
   - **What to Show:** *How data enters the system -- upload, form, API call*
   - **What to Say:** *"The user provides [type of input]. Notice how [key feature] makes this easy."*
   - **Transition:** *"Watch what happens when we submit this."*

3. **Step 3: *Show processing in action***
   - **What to Show:** *The system processing the input -- loading states, pipeline stages*
   - **What to Say:** *"Behind the scenes, [describe what the system is doing]. This takes [time] compared to [old method time]."*
   - **Transition:** *"Here are the results."*

4. **Step 4: *Display results***
   - **What to Show:** *The output, analysis, or recommendations generated*
   - **What to Say:** *"The system has [describe output]. Notice [key insight or feature of the results]."*
   - **Transition:** *"Let me show you how this integrates with their existing workflow."*

5. **Step 5: *Demonstrate integration***
   - **What to Show:** *How the solution connects to existing systems*
   - **What to Say:** *"This connects directly to their [system], so results flow automatically into [downstream process]."*
   - **Transition:** *"Now let's look at an edge case."*

6. **Step 6: *Handle an edge case***
   - **What to Show:** *An unusual input or error scenario and how the system handles it*
   - **What to Say:** *"When we encounter [edge case], the system [describe graceful handling]. This was a specific concern from the customer."*
   - **Transition:** *"Let me show you the monitoring side."*

7. **Step 7: *Show monitoring and observability***
   - **What to Show:** *Dashboard, logs, or metrics showing system performance*
   - **What to Say:** *"We can track [key metrics] in real time. Right now you can see [specific metric values]."*
   - **Transition:** *"Let me zoom out and show you the impact."*

8. **Step 8: *Present before/after comparison***
   - **What to Show:** *Side-by-side or sequential comparison of old vs. new*
   - **What to Say:** *"Before this solution, [old state]. Now, [new state]. That's a [X%] improvement in [metric]."*
   - **Transition:** *"Let me walk through the full results."*

9. **Step 9: *Highlight a differentiating feature***
   - **What to Show:** *A feature that sets this solution apart*
   - **What to Say:** *"What makes this approach unique is [differentiator]. This is possible because [technical reason]."*
   - **Transition:** *"Let me wrap up with the overall impact."*

10. **Step 10: *Show the complete workflow end-to-end***
    - **What to Show:** *A quick replay or summary view of the full workflow*
    - **What to Say:** *"So in summary, the complete flow goes from [start] to [end] in [time], fully automated."*
    - **Transition:** *"Let me share the numbers."*

## Technical Deep Dive (if asked)

*Prepare 3 technical topics you can go deeper on if the audience asks.*

### Topic 1: *AI/ML Model Selection and Performance*
- **Key Points:** *Why this model was chosen, how it was fine-tuned or prompted, accuracy metrics*
- **Diagram/Visual to Show:** *Model architecture diagram, benchmark comparison table*

### Topic 2: *System Architecture and Scalability*
- **Key Points:** *How the system handles scale, infrastructure choices, performance optimizations*
- **Diagram/Visual to Show:** *Architecture diagram, load testing results*

### Topic 3: *Data Pipeline and Security*
- **Key Points:** *How data flows through the system, encryption, access controls, compliance*
- **Diagram/Visual to Show:** *Data flow diagram, security architecture overview*

## Q&A Prep

### Question 1: *"How does this handle [scale/volume] in production?"*
- **Answer:** *Describe your scaling strategy and any load testing results*
- **Supporting Evidence:** *Reference benchmark data, architecture decisions, or infrastructure specs*

### Question 2: *"What happens when the model makes a mistake?"*
- **Answer:** *Describe your error handling, human-in-the-loop processes, and fallback mechanisms*
- **Supporting Evidence:** *Reference accuracy metrics, confidence thresholds, and monitoring alerts*

### Question 3: *"How long would it take to deploy this in production?"*
- **Answer:** *Provide a realistic timeline with phases and milestones*
- **Supporting Evidence:** *Reference your deployment plan and similar past implementations*

### Question 4: *"What is the total cost of ownership?"*
- **Answer:** *Break down infrastructure, licensing, maintenance, and personnel costs*
- **Supporting Evidence:** *Reference your ROI analysis and cost projections*

### Question 5: *"How does this compare to [alternative solution]?"*
- **Answer:** *Provide an honest comparison highlighting your differentiation*
- **Supporting Evidence:** *Reference specific technical or business advantages with data*

---

## Closing and Next Steps

*Summarize what was shown, restate the value, and propose clear next steps.*

> *"Today I showed you how [solution name] solves [customer]'s [problem] by [approach]. The results speak for themselves: [headline metric improvement]. Here's what I recommend as next steps."*

- *Schedule a technical review with the customer's engineering team by [date]*
- *Finalize the production deployment plan and timeline*
- *Define the success criteria and measurement plan for the pilot*
- *Identify the internal champion and executive sponsor for the rollout*
- *Set a 30-day check-in to review initial production metrics*
