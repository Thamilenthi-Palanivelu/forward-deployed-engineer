## Presentation Tips

- **Lead with the business problem, not the technology.** Your audience cares about impact first. The technology is how you got there, not why they should care.
- **Practice your demo at least 3 times before presenting.** Have a backup plan (screenshots or video) in case something breaks live. Nothing kills credibility faster than a failed demo.
- **Use concrete numbers, not vague claims.** "Reduced processing time from 4 hours to 12 minutes" is far more compelling than "significantly improved performance."
- **Anticipate the skeptic in the room.** Prepare honest answers for tough questions. Saying "I don't know, but here's how I'd find out" is better than bluffing.
- **End with a clear call to action.** Don't let the presentation fizzle out. Tell the audience exactly what should happen next and who should own it.
