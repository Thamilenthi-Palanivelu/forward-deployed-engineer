## Key Lessons

1. *Lesson 1: What did you learn about working with customers? -- e.g., "Discovery is not a phase, it's continuous. The most important requirements surfaced during the build, not the initial interviews."*
2. *Lesson 2: What did you learn about building solutions? -- e.g., "Start with the simplest version that demonstrates value. The customer was more impressed by a working prototype than a polished design document."*
3. *Lesson 3: What did you learn about AI/ML in production? -- e.g., "Model accuracy is necessary but not sufficient. The post-processing and error handling around the model were just as important as the model itself."*
4. *Lesson 4: What did you learn about communication? -- e.g., "Technical depth earns trust, but business impact closes deals. Always translate technical achievements into business language."*
5. *Lesson 5: What would you do differently? -- e.g., "I would have involved the customer's ops team earlier. They had critical context about edge cases that I discovered too late."*
