## Negotiation Talking Points

2. *Unique value -- Articulate what you bring that justifies your target range. Tie to business impact.*
3. *Competing offers -- How will you leverage other offers or interest without bluffing?*
4. *Non-salary items -- What levers beyond base salary are you prepared to negotiate on?*
5. *Walk-away point -- What is your minimum acceptable offer? Know this before the conversation starts.*

## Non-Salary Considerations

## Questions to Ask About Compensation

*Prepare 3-4 questions to ask during the compensation discussion.*

1. *What is the equity refresh policy, and how are refreshers determined?*
2. *How is the bonus structured, and what are typical payout percentages?*
3. *What does the promotion and compensation review cycle look like?*
4. *Are there additional benefits or perks not listed in the offer letter?*
