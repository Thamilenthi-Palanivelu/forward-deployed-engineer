**Support Level Key:**
- **Champion** = Actively supports and advocates for the project
- **Neutral** = Neither for nor against; needs convincing
- **Skeptic** = Has concerns or actively resists; needs specific engagement

### Quadrant Strategies

| Quadrant | Strategy | Communication Frequency | Communication Type |
|----------|----------|------------------------|-------------------|
| **Manage Closely** (High Power, High Interest) | *Active engagement, regular updates, involve in decisions* | *Weekly or more* | *1:1 meetings, detailed reports* |
| **Keep Satisfied** (High Power, Low Interest) | *Provide enough info to keep satisfied, no overwhelming detail* | *Bi-weekly to monthly* | *Executive summaries, milestone updates* |
| **Keep Informed** (Low Power, High Interest) | *Regular communication, leverage their enthusiasm* | *Weekly* | *Team updates, demos, feedback sessions* |
| **Monitor** (Low Power, Low Interest) | *Minimal effort, general updates* | *Monthly or as needed* | *Newsletter, all-hands updates* |
