# Technical Stories (STAR Format)

**Date:** *YYYY-MM-DD*

*Prepare 5 polished technical stories using the STAR format. Each story should demonstrate a different FDE-relevant competency.*

---

## Story 1: *[Title]*

**Theme:** Technical Challenge

### Situation

*Set the scene. What was the context? Who was involved? What was the environment -- customer engagement, internal project, production incident? Provide enough detail for the interviewer to understand the stakes.*

### Task

*What was your specific responsibility or goal? What were you expected to deliver and within what constraints (timeline, resources, technical limitations)?*

### Action

*What did you do? Be specific about YOUR actions, not the team's. Walk through your decision-making process, the technical approach you took, and any pivots you made along the way.*

### Result

*What was the outcome? Quantify where possible: revenue impact, performance improvement, time saved, customer satisfaction score, adoption numbers. What changed as a result of your work?*

### FDE Relevance

*What does this story demonstrate about your readiness for an FDE role? Connect it to core FDE competencies: technical depth, customer empathy, speed of execution, operating in ambiguity.*
