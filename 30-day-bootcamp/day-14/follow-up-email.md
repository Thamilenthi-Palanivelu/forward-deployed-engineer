# Post-Meeting Follow-Up Email

> Send this within 24 hours of any significant meeting -- demo, review, decision point, or stakeholder check-in. A good follow-up email turns a conversation into a commitment.

---

## Template

**Subject Line:** *[Project Name] -- [Meeting Type] Follow-Up and Action Items*

*[e.g., "ShipTrack POC -- Demo Follow-Up and Action Items"]*

---

**Body:**

> Hi *[Name(s)]*,
>
> Thank you for your time today -- *[personalize with one specific reference to the meeting, e.g., "the questions your team raised about notification timing were exactly the right ones to be asking at this stage"]*.
>
> Here is a summary of our discussion so we have a shared record.
>
> ---
>
> **Key Decisions Made:**
>
> - *[e.g., "Agreed to proceed with a 4-week production build following the successful POC demo"]*
> - *[e.g., "Confirmed that the notification threshold will be set to 60 minutes (delay > 1 hour triggers alert)"]*
> - *[e.g., "Decided to pilot with the East Coast CS team first (12 reps) before full rollout"]*
> - *[Decision 4]*
>
> ---
>
> **Action Items:**
>
> | #  | Action Item                                      | Owner              | Deadline           |
> |----|--------------------------------------------------|--------------------|--------------------|
> | 1  | *[e.g., "Send production build proposal and timeline"]* | *[e.g., "Your Name"]* | *[e.g., "Friday Oct 18"]* |
> | 2  | *[e.g., "Provide production AWS credentials"]*   | *[e.g., "Client IT"]* | *[e.g., "Monday Oct 21"]* |
> | 3  | *[e.g., "Identify pilot CS reps and schedule training"]* | *[e.g., "CS Manager"]* | *[e.g., "Wednesday Oct 23"]* |
> | 4  | *[e.g., "Share customer contact list for notifications"]* | *[e.g., "Client Ops"]* | *[e.g., "Friday Oct 18"]* |
> | 5  | *[Action item]*                                  | *[Owner]*          | *[Deadline]*       |
>
> ---
>
> **Next Steps:**
>
> - *[e.g., "I will send the production build proposal by Friday for your review"]*
> - *[e.g., "We will schedule a 30-minute kickoff for the production phase next week"]*
> - *[e.g., "I will follow up on the open questions from today (see below) by [date]"]*
>
> ---
>
> **Open Questions (I will follow up on these):**
>
> - *[e.g., "What is the latency impact of adding end-to-end encryption? (Answer by Oct 18)"]*
> - *[e.g., "Can we get read access to the carrier's real-time webhook? (Checking with carrier contact)"]*
> - *[Open question]*
>
> ---
>
> Please let me know if I have missed anything or if any of the above does not match your understanding. I want to make sure we are fully aligned.
>
> Looking forward to the next phase.
>
> Best,
> *[Your Name]*
> *[Your Title]*
> *[Your Phone/Slack -- make it easy for them to reach you]*

## Customization Notes

### For a Demo Follow-Up

Add this section after the opening:

> **What We Demonstrated:**
> - *[Feature 1 and key result -- e.g., "Real-time shipment lookup: reduced from 15 minutes to 2 seconds"]*
> - *[Feature 2 and key result -- e.g., "Automated delay notification: email delivered within 3 minutes of delay detection"]*
> - *[Key metric -- e.g., "System processed 2,400 live shipments during the demo with 99.2% data accuracy"]*

### For a Decision Meeting Follow-Up

Add this section:

> **Decision Summary:**
> - **Decision:** *[e.g., "Proceed with production build"]*
> - **Rationale:** *[e.g., "POC met all 3 success criteria; stakeholders confirmed business value"]*
> - **Conditions:** *[e.g., "Subject to IT security review approval by Oct 25"]*
> - **Escalation path if conditions are not met:** *[e.g., "Reconnect with VP Ops to reassess timeline"]*

### For a Difficult Meeting Follow-Up

If the meeting surfaced concerns or conflicts, lead with acknowledgment:

> I appreciate the candid feedback today. I want to address the concerns raised:
>
> - **Concern: *[e.g., "Data accuracy needs to be higher before pilot"]***
>   Response: *[e.g., "Agreed. I will implement additional validation rules this week and share updated accuracy metrics by Friday."]*
>
> - **Concern: *[e.g., "Timeline feels aggressive"]***
>   Response: *[e.g., "I have re-evaluated the timeline and believe it is achievable, but I have identified [X] as the highest risk item. I will flag immediately if it slips."]*

---

## Follow-Up Email Checklist

- [ ] Sent within 24 hours of the meeting
- [ ] Subject line includes the project name and meeting type
- [ ] Includes at least one personalized reference to the conversation
- [ ] All decisions are stated clearly and unambiguously
- [ ] Every action item has an owner AND a deadline
- [ ] Open questions have a committed follow-up date
- [ ] Tone is professional but warm -- not robotic
- [ ] Proofread for typos and correct names/titles
- [ ] CC'd only the people who were in the meeting (or need to know)
- [ ] No sensitive information that should not be in email

---

*The follow-up email is the most underrated tool in an FDE's arsenal. It turns verbal agreements into written commitments and shows the client you are organized, reliable, and paying attention.*
