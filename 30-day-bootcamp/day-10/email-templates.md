# Professional Email Templates

> These three templates cover the most common emails an FDE sends during an engagement. Customize them for your situation, but preserve the structure -- it works because it respects the reader's time while keeping momentum.

---

## Template 1: Post-Discovery Follow-Up

*Send within 24 hours of a discovery meeting. Demonstrates professionalism and locks in shared understanding.*

---

**Subject Line:** *[Your Company] + [Client Company] -- Discovery Follow-Up and Next Steps*

**Body:**

> Hi *[Client Name]*,
>
> Thank you for taking the time to walk us through *[topic of discovery -- e.g., "your shipment tracking operations"]* today. It was a productive conversation and we appreciate your team's openness.
>
> Here is my summary of what we discussed:
>
> **Key Takeaways:**
> - *[e.g., "Your CS team spends approximately 75 hours/week responding to shipment status inquiries across three disconnected systems"]*
> - *[e.g., "The primary goal is to reduce response time and improve customer satisfaction before your largest account's Q4 renewal"]*
> - *[e.g., "Any solution must integrate with your existing SAP warehouse system and Zendesk CS platform"]*
>
> **What We Heard as Priorities:**
> 1. *[Priority 1]*
> 2. *[Priority 2]*
> 3. *[Priority 3]*
>
> **Agreed Next Steps:**
>
> | Action Item                              | Owner               | Target Date       |
> |------------------------------------------|----------------------|-------------------|
> | *[e.g., "Share solution brief"]*          | *[e.g., "Our team"]* | *[e.g., "Friday"]* |
> | *[e.g., "Provide API documentation"]*     | *[e.g., "Client IT"]* | *[e.g., "Next Tuesday"]* |
> | *[e.g., "Schedule technical deep-dive"]*  | *[e.g., "Joint"]*   | *[e.g., "Next week"]* |
>
> Please let me know if I have missed anything or if any of the above does not match your understanding. I want to make sure we are aligned before we move forward.
>
> Looking forward to the next conversation.
>
> Best,
> *[Your Name]*

**Call to Action:** *Confirm the summary is accurate and the next steps are agreed upon.*

## Template 2: POC Kick-Off Communication

*Send at the start of the POC to align all stakeholders on scope, timeline, and expectations.*

---

**Subject Line:** *[Project Name] POC -- Kick-Off Summary and Timeline*

**Body:**

> Hi everyone,
>
> Thank you for joining today's POC kick-off. I am excited to get started. Below is a summary of what we aligned on, so we have a single reference point as we move forward.
>
> **POC Objective:**
> *[e.g., "Validate that a unified tracking dashboard can reduce shipment status lookup time from 15 minutes to under 30 seconds, using live data from your warehouse and carrier systems."]*
>
> **Scope:**
> - **In scope:** *[e.g., "Real-time tracking dashboard, automated delay notifications"]*
> - **Out of scope:** *[e.g., "Predictive ETA, driver mobile app, analytics dashboard (planned for Phase 2)"]*
>
> **Timeline:**
>
> | Week   | Focus                               | Milestone                           |
> |--------|-------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
> | Week 1 | *[e.g., "Setup and data integration"]* | *[e.g., "Live data flowing"]*    |
> | Week 2 | *[e.g., "Core dashboard build"]*    | *[e.g., "Internal demo"]*          |
> | Week 3 | *[e.g., "Notifications + polish"]*  | *[e.g., "Stakeholder demo"]*       |
>
> **What We Need From Your Team:**
> - *[e.g., "API credentials for WMS and carrier systems (by [date])"]*
> - *[e.g., "30 minutes with a CS rep for user workflow review (this week)"]*
> - *[e.g., "A designated point of contact for technical questions"]*
>
> **Communication Cadence:**
> - *[e.g., "Weekly status email every Friday"]*
> - *[e.g., "Slack channel [#channel-name] for day-to-day questions"]*
> - *[e.g., "Bi-weekly stakeholder check-in (calendar invite to follow)"]*
>
> **Success Criteria:**
> 1. *[e.g., "Shipment status lookup in < 30 seconds"]*
> 2. *[e.g., "Delay notifications sent within 5 minutes of detection"]*
> 3. *[e.g., "Positive feedback from 3+ CS reps in pilot"]*
>
> If anything above does not match your expectations, please flag it now -- it is much easier to adjust scope at the start than midway through.
>
> Let's build something great.
>
> Best,
> *[Your Name]*

**Call to Action:** *Confirm scope and timeline; flag any concerns immediately.*

## Template 3: Status Update to Stakeholders

*Send weekly. Keep it short. Executives scan, they do not read.*

---

**Subject Line:** *[Project Name] -- Week [#] Status Update*

**Body:**

> Hi *[Stakeholder Names or "team"]*,
>
> Here is this week's update on *[Project Name]*.
>
> **Status: [On Track / At Risk / Blocked]** *[Use one of these -- be honest]*
>
> **What We Accomplished This Week:**
> - *[e.g., "Completed integration with warehouse management API -- live data is now flowing"]*
> - *[e.g., "Built the shipment search and status detail pages"]*
> - *[e.g., "Ran initial performance tests -- API response time averaging 120ms"]*
>
> **What's Planned for Next Week:**
> - *[e.g., "Build notification engine for delay alerts"]*
> - *[e.g., "Begin pilot testing with 2 CS reps"]*
> - *[e.g., "Prepare demo for stakeholder review on [date]"]*
>
> **Blockers / Risks:**
>
> | Issue                                     | Impact                           | What We Need                    |
> |-------------------------------------------|----------------------------------|---------------------------------|
> | *[e.g., "Carrier API credentials delayed"]* | *[e.g., "Cannot test GPS data"]* | *[e.g., "Credentials by Wed"]* |
> | *[e.g., "None this week"]*                | --                               | --                              |
>
> **Key Metric:**
> *[e.g., "Shipment lookup time: 15 min (before) vs. 8 sec (current POC) -- 99% improvement"]*
>
> Happy to jump on a call if any of this raises questions. Otherwise, we are heads-down building.
>
> Best,
> *[Your Name]*

**Call to Action:** *Unblock any listed blockers; otherwise, no action needed (respect their time).*

## Email Best Practices for FDEs

- **Send within 24 hours** of any meeting or milestone
- **Bold the status** -- make it impossible to miss
- **Use tables** for action items -- they are scannable
- **Keep it under 300 words** -- if you need more, attach a document
- **Always end with a clear ask** -- what do you need from the reader?
- **CC sparingly** -- only include people who need to act or be informed
- **Proofread once** -- typos erode the trust you are building
