## Bootcamp Overview

The 30-Day FDE Bootcamp is structured into four weeks plus a two-day capstone:

- **Week 1 (Days 1-7): Foundation** -- Self-assessment, role understanding, technical baseline
- **Week 2 (Days 8-14): Customer Skills** -- Discovery, communication, demos, objection handling
- **Week 3 (Days 15-21): Solution Design & Deployment** -- Architecture, AI patterns, production readiness, adoption
- **Week 4 (Days 22-28): Strategy, Industry, and Interview Prep** -- Industry expertise, personal branding, interview readiness
- **Capstone (Days 29-30): Capstone Project** -- End-to-end FDE engagement simulation
